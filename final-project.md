## Checkpoints

### Initial Survey
In the initial survey, please provide information about your working style and preferences. Halie will do her best to give you a group that will allow you to work at your best.

### Group Contract
The group contract allows you to make a plan with your group to ensure you all have a good experience. We ask you to:

- Define your team members, roles, and responsibilities.
- Outline communication expectations and meeting schedules.
- Establish initial project goals and expectations.

### Initial Brainstorm
Here we ask you to:
- develop an initial set of ideas and possible solutions for your stakeholder.
- identify key challenges and potential database requirements.
- prepare questions for your first stakeholder meeting.

### Project Proposal 
The main components of this proposal are the following:

- **Schema Draft**: A preliminary design of your database structure, including entities, relationships, and key attributes.
- **Stakeholder Check-in Notes**: A summary of your meetings with the stakeholder, detailing their requirements, constraints, and feedback.
- **Project Timeline**: A structured breakdown of your project phases and expected milestones.

### Team Dynamics Check-in
This short Google form is a mid-project check-in about how everything is going with your team. It should be completed individually and reflect on both your personal contributions and general experiences in the group.

### Final Project Submission + Demo
Your presentation is a chance to showcase your project to your classmates and stakeholders. We are asking you to record a video. It should be around 10-12 minutes.
You can find details about the presentation requirements at this link or on Moodle.
Please submit your presentation on Moodle by the end of finals!

### Final Check-in Survey 
Please fill out this final check-in survey about your experiences in this class and with the final project. This survey is individual and confidential.
