# Previous Final Projects

Here are a sampling of final projects from prior years! We started doing project-based learning in Spring 2025.
 
# 2025

[Equipment Loan Database](https://github.com/bjalloh910/EquipmentLoan-Project)
Team: Bintu, Rehab, Fatima and Nafisa
Stakeholder: Kala'i Ellis of the [Smith Spatial Analysis Lab](https://www.smith.edu/discover-smith/smith-action/sustainable-smith/spatial-analysis-lab)
Goal: To create a reservation system for the equipment managed by the SAL, such as GPS, drones, rangefinders, etc.

[Frog Foot Flagging Behavior in DeeplabCut](https://github.com/kwilson44/DeepLabCut-Foot-Flagging)
[Video Demo](https://www.awesomescreenshot.com/video/39531769?key=6c213b2bcadbe29e488d1705bbc2210b)
Team: Katie & Nida (+ others who didn't list their names publicly)
Stakeholder: [Prof. Lisa Mangiamele, Smith BIO department](https://www.smith.edu/people/lisa-mangiamele)
Goal: To make it easier to track foot flagging behavior in frogs by allowing spatial queries with PostgreSQL

[Mothitor Light Monitoring](https://github.com/CDarling25/mothitor_light)
Team: Cameron, Kika, Syd, Molly, Caroline
Stakeholder: [Prof. Mariana Abarca, Smith BIO department](https://www.smith.edu/people/mariana-abarca)
Goal: To integrate multiple data sources with data collected by a light monitor to contextualize moth behavior as captured by the [Mothitor](https://www.smith.edu/news-events/news/butterflies-and-moths-changing-climate)

[Drone Imagery Search System](https://github.com/Zoe12341/drones/tree/main)
Team: Sonora, Zoe, Glenvelis, Casey
Stakeholder: Kala'i Ellis of the [Smith Spatial Analysis Lab](https://www.smith.edu/discover-smith/smith-action/sustainable-smith/spatial-analysis-lab)
Goal: To store spatial information from drone flights to allow location-based querying
